3. Mic Button Revert

The mic reverts when onSTTStopped fires. This happens:

  • ~2.5s after you stop speaking (silenceAfterSpeechToStopMicMs)
  • ~10s silence (closing message, then stop)
  • On error (STT/WebSocket fails)
  • When you click mic again to stop

Open the main app with ?debug=1 and watch the console for [JARVIS] [TRACE] entries. Look for: Mic clicked, startSTT, VAD onSpeechEnd, onTranscript, and when onSTTStopped would fire.
